## Active research

### 🫀 Hidden AF after Stroke — Sinus-Rhythm 1D ECG
Predicting **occult atrial fibrillation** in ischaemic stroke patients from a single normal (sinus-rhythm) 12-lead ECG, using public **MIMIC-IV-ECG** data.

- 1D CNN / ResNet on raw waveforms vs P-wave feature baselines
- **Label-noise-robust (positive-unlabelled) learning**, since "no AF" patients were never monitored long-term
- **Calibration** and **fairness audit** by sex, age and race
- Status: 🟡 in progress · target: *PLOS Digital Health*

### 🧠 Brain Clot, Edema & Midline Shift
Detection and measurement of **intracranial clot, oedema and midline shift** from brain imaging.
Repo: [Brain-Clot-Edema-midlineshift](https://github.com/ChanduVenkatSaiNarne/Brain-Clot-Edema-midlineshift) *(private — available on request)*

### 🧠 Gaussian Splatting for Brain MRI
Reconstructing high-resolution adult brain MRI from fewer slices with Gaussian Splatting, and checking whether **real anatomy** (cortical thickness, hippocampal volume) is preserved — designed to run on an **8 GB GPU**.
Status: 🟡 early stage
